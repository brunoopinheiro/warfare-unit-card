Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Warfare Unit Card is a free, front-end only tool for building unit cards for Kingdoms & Warfare. There are no accounts, and the app has no server of its own that receives the cards you make. This page explains what data is involved when you use the site.

Cards you create

The unit cards you save to the Library are stored only in your own browser, using its local storage. They are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time with "Delete" or "Clear library", or by clearing your browser's site data. Downloaded PNG images are created in your browser and saved to your device.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy and cookie settings" link that appears on the site.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel processes technical request data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

External links

Links to Kingdoms & Warfare, LinkedIn, GitHub and Ko-fi take you to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the site changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. For questions, reach out on LinkedIn or GitHub.
